## 0.1.0 — 2026-08-24

### Added

- Vendor-neutral capability-based routing policy with FAST, BALANCED, DEEP, CRITICAL, and SPECIALIST modes.
- Fail-closed capability fallback, cost approval boundaries, and non-sensitive decision-record contract.
- Deterministic package validation, routing fixtures, and forward-test evidence.
- Exact full-route outcome classification, machine-enforced adversarial safety constraints, and a deterministically reconciled confusion/variance report.
- Versioned adapter manifests and a separately configurable execution-budget policy.
- Release, compatibility, contribution, rollout, rollback, and security guidance.
- Hash-locked development analyzers, checksum-verified workflow tooling, and least-privilege CI quality gates.

### Changed

- Initial installations require explicit `$auto-agent` invocation. Implicit invocation is a later, project-specific opt-in.
